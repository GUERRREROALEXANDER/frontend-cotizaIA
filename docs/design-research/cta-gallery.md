# CTA Gallery (https://www.cta.gallery/)
## What it is (2-3 lines)
Curated gallery of 200+ call-to-action examples categorized by type (Button, Call-to-Buy, Download, Form, Modal/Pop-up, Navigation, Newsletter, Pricing/Subscription) and filterable by industry/mode. Each entry links to a detail page with screenshot and context.

## Free or paid (what is free, license if stated)
Free to browse. Sponsored by Webestica (Webflow/Framer templates, Iconstica icons, Bootstrap templates). No license stated for gallery content.
