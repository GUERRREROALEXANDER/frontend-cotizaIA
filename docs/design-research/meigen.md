# MeiGen (https://www.meigen.ai/?category=product)
## What it is (2-3 lines)
Free AI prompt gallery for image/video generation (GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana, Seedance 2.5). Categories: Ads & Product, Brand & Logo, Videos, Illustration & 3D, Posters & Visuals, Portraits, Storyboard & Characters, Wallpaper. Drag-and-drop prompt cards to apply.

## Free or paid (what is free, license if stated)
Free to use. "Free AI Prompts Gallery" — no license stated. Also offers ChatGPT Plugin, MCP Server, Mobile App.
