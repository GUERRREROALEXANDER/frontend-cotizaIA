# Webestica (https://www.webestica.com/)
## What it is (2-3 lines)
A template shop selling premium website templates for Framer, Webflow, Bootstrap, Tailwind, and Figma. The catalog is organized by platform and business domain (SaaS, agency, consulting, AI startup), each template shipping section-based pages with CMS-ready structure.
## Free or paid (what is free, license if stated)
Paid. Tailwind templates are $49 each (e.g. https://www.webestica.com/tailwind-templates); Bootstrap templates $49; Webflow templates sold individually or via an "All-Access Pass" (320+ templates). Free template samplers exist at https://www.webestica.com/free-webflow-templates and free Figma cloneables; Iconstica (700+ icons) is listed as free. No full license text was read on the fetched pages.
