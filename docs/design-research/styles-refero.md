# Refero Styles (https://styles.refero.design)
## What it is (2-3 lines)
A gallery of 2,000+ AI-readable design systems captured from leading product websites. Each entry exposes colors, typography, spacing, components, and an exportable DESIGN.md meant to be pasted into Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, v0, or Lovable.
## Free or paid (what is free, license if stated)
Browsing the gallery and opening individual style pages appears free; no license text was visible on the fetched pages. A "Connect to your AI" banner points to a paid/external MCP integration at https://refero.design/mcp. No per-template price was shown on the fetched pages.
## Steal for CotizaIA (5-10 bullets: concrete, named pieces usable in a dashboard: nav/sidebar, KPI cards, tables, empty states, CTAs, forms, toasts, command palettes, motion recipes with durations/easings if given. Each bullet with a direct URL when one exists)
- DESIGN.md token contract (colors by role + type rules + spacing) as the single source of truth for CotizaIA dark + light themes: copy the format from https://styles.refero.design/ai-agents/design-md-examples

- Warm-paper dashboard skin (Notion/Perplexity-style parchment background behind clean cards) for the brief-paste form to feel less sterile: https://styles.refero.design/style/2bf4c61f-de10-4614-ba1b-20c0453bd2a9
- Serif-analytics KPI card style (Steep: serif numerals on warm paper) for quote totals / margin / win-rate cards: https://styles.refero.design/style/75fdb89f-ca64-41b3-af36-7a78bd09448e
- Single-accent CTA rule (Brex/Freitag pattern: neutral concrete canvas, one ember/yellow reserved strictly for the primary action like "Generate quote"): https://styles.refero.design/style/b58d92f6-68a8-4358-8fc9-6ea58e6d483b
- Color-by-function discipline (canvas vs. content surface vs. readable text vs. selected state vs. action) applied to CotizaIA status pills (draft / generating / sent / accepted): https://styles.refero.design/ai-agents/design-md-examples
- No motion recipes with durations/easings were published on the fetched pages, so no motion values are copied here.

## Skip (what does not fit a dashboard, 1-3 bullets)
- Editorial/brand-showcase skins (matte-black poster, music brand, broadsheet magazine) — too expressive for a dense quoting dashboard.
- Product-imagery-driven styles (Zara/Nike gallery walls) — CotizaIA is data and forms, not hero photography.
